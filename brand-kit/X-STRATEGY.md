## 1. Who we are talking to

1. **Indie and small-business builders.** They respond to build-in-public posts about how a one-person studio ships many apps.
2. **App users:**
   - Windows users who care about privacy (Vox Reader, Cropmark, Still-Lane)
   - Parents and teachers (Visual Schedule Board)
   - Seniors and their carers (Large Print Crosswords)
   - Home cooks (Recipe Box)
3. **Nigerian tech and business.** CompoundOS for estates, plus the "made in Nigeria" story. This is the strongest local angle, with little competition.

## 2. Content pillars (in rotation)

| Pillar | Share | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Build in public** | 35% | "Shipped Large Print Crosswords v1.2: free with a one-time unlock. Here's why we moved off paid-upfront." Include numbers: downloads, reviews, what failed. |
| **Show the product** | 25% | A 15–30 s screen recording of one feature, such as Cropmark redacting a screenshot. Video always beats a still. |
| **Useful tips** | 20% | Short threads made from your site guides: "How to permanently redact a screenshot", "Visual schedules for autistic children". Each one links back to the guide. |
| **Local / CompoundOS** | 10% | Estate-management problems in Nigeria and how gate passes or service charges get solved. Tag Nigerian proptech and real-estate voices. |
| **Personality** | 10% | The studio, the process, opinions on software built to last. |

You already have 20+ long-form guides on the site, and each one is a ready-made thread. That's roughly two months of content before you need any new ideas.

## 3. Cadence

- **Posts:** 1 a day on weekdays, at about 8–9 am or 6–8 pm WAT. That catches Nigeria and the UK, and US mornings in the evening slot.
- **Threads and videos:** 2 threads and 1 video a week.
- **Replies:** 20–30 minutes a day replying thoughtfully to bigger accounts in your niches. At 0 followers, replies get far more reach than your own posts. Say something useful; never just "great post".
- **Batching:** write a week of posts on Sunday and schedule them in X's built-in scheduler, under Post, then the clock icon.

## 4. Who to follow (replace the 17)

Follow 50–100 accounts:

- Windows and Microsoft Store developers
- Amazon Appstore and Fire tablet developers
- the autism and special-education community, for Visual Schedule Board
- Nigerian tech: founders, Techpoint, TechCabal, Nigerian dev communities

## 5. Funnel

- **Clicks:** each post that links to the site uses a link with tracking tags, such as `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<topic>`. Clarity (with consent) and Search Console then show what X sends.
- **Placement:** put the link in the first reply rather than the main post, because X shows posts with links to fewer people.
- **Website:** the footer and schema now link to X (done).
