Large Print Crosswords: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

Large Print Crosswords collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app itself never connects to the internet.

Purchases

The app is free to try. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the full puzzle library. Amazon handles that purchase through the Amazon Appstore on your tablet, under Amazon's own privacy notice. Buying or restoring the unlock needs an internet connection. We receive no payment details and no personal information from the purchase; the app only learns whether the unlock is owned.

What the app stores

The app saves two things on your tablet so that it can pick up where you left off: your puzzle progress, and your display choices (text size, colors and key sound). This information stays on your tablet. It is never sent anywhere, and we cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, a name or an email address.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It contains no third-party services that collect data.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your tablet's settings, deletes everything it has saved.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
