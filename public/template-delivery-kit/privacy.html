Privacy Policy

Template Delivery Kit (Chrome extension) · Last updated 13 November 2026

Template Delivery Kit helps sellers of digital templates make branded delivery PDFs and check that their template links still open for buyers. It has no account system and no server of its own. This page lists everything it stores and everything it sends.

What stays on your computer

These are saved only in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leave your computer:

  • Your brand settings: shop name, logo, colours, instructions, licence terms and support email.
  • Your product list: product names, template links and the result of each link check.
  • Your licence key and its activation ID, if you bought the paid plan.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What is sent, and where

WhenSent toWhat is sent
You click "Check all links"canva.com, docs.google.com or notion.siteA request for each template link you saved, made without your login or cookies, to see whether it still opens. Nothing else is attached.
You activate, renew or remove a licence (and once a day while a licence is active)Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com)Your licence key and the activation ID. Lemon Squeezy answers whether the licence is valid.

Google and Notion access is only requested the first time you check a link on those sites, and you can refuse it.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No browsing history. The extension reads the current tab's address only when you click "Use link from this tab".
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Your name, email and payment details go to Lemon Squeezy under their privacy policy. The extension never sees them.
